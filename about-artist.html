About The Artist

ARTIST HISTORY

Ricardo Horacio Iorio (Ciudadela, Tres de Febrero, Buenos Aires; June 25, 1962 - Coronel Suárez, Buenos Aires; October 24, 2023)1​2​ was an Argentine musician and producer, known for being the founder of the bands V8, Hermética and Almafuerte. He is considered one of the main references and promoters of Argentine heavy metal.3​4​5​6​ He began his musical career as bassist for V8, a group he founded with Ricardo "Chofa" Moreno. After publishing three albums and some changes of members, they separated in 1987. In 1988 he formed Hermética, where in addition to being the bassist, he became the main musical composer and exclusive composer of the lyrics, as well as singing occasionally. After the band's rise in 1994, it broke up. In early 1995 he formed Almafuerte, where he established himself as a singer and bassist. He would abandon the bass in 2002 and in 2016 he announced the dissolution of Almafuerte. His career consisted of seventeen studio albums, various live albums and numerous participations with artists such as Pappo, León Gieco, Claudio Marciello or Flavio Cianciarulo, among others.

For more than four decades, Iorio has inspired several generations of fans, becoming a fundamental figure of rock culture in the country. His album ‘Tangos y Milongas’ is a clear example of his versatility and his respect for traditional Argentine music.
